# Part III — Finite-Domain Quantified Logic

Collection: DR0149-r3, 9 November 2026. Author: Trinh Tung Lam.
Contact: lamtung0481@gmail.com.

The mathematical statements and proof arguments of the v0.7.1 finite-domain
paper are retained. This edition updates the cover, artifact pointers, relation
to later equality results, and removes a dated priority claim. It is therefore
a revised presentation, not the byte-identical archived PDF.

The paper develops the function-free finite-domain quantified calculus, crisp
equality, macro-free extensional tableau, grounding and repaired replay.
Part I supplies the project overview; Part II the propositional foundation.
Infinite-domain pure equality does not imply unrestricted quantified completeness.
