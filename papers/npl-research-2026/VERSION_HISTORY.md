# Version history and reading map

## DR0149-r3 — 9 November 2026

Collection title: Nullivance Logic: Foundations, Quantified Reasoning, and Certified Recognition.

All three current PDFs and their source metadata use this collection identity.
Part I is a rewritten overview and recognition paper, with questions, contribution
boundaries, related work, examples, proofs and limitations. Part II is the revised
propositional paper. Part III is the finite-domain quantified paper with unchanged
mathematics and updated presentation. This is an editorial and verification
revision of the existing DR-0149 mathematical baseline, not a new mathematical
milestone or a new software API version. No v0.7.2 Git tag is implied.

Part II already differed materially from its archived v0.7.1 PDF in the r2 ZIP:
FDE conservativity, bilattice collapse, and complexity proof components had been
formalized. Its old cover label was misleading. This edition corrects that label
and explains the exact formalization boundary. Part III no longer asserts priority
on the basis of an August literature search. Collection revision does not mean
that every mathematical result was discovered in November.

## Original version 1 — exploratory foundations

https://zenodo.org/records/18309849

Historical motivation and exploratory formulation. Broad physical/cognitive
examples and comparisons there are not promoted to verified results by later
Lean work. Current alpha/Theta quasivance uses exact zero intensity and nonneutral
structure, not a general low-intensity heuristic. Two support channels are not
complementary probabilities. The model does not prove that an unmanifested entity
physically influences reality. Classical mathematics is not claimed unable to
represent continuous or uncertain data.

## How to cite and reproduce

The concept DOI 10.5281/zenodo.18309848 identifies the version family.
Choose the record explicitly labeled DR0149-r3 when citing this collection,
and identify the relevant Part I, II or III. SOURCE_MANIFEST.sha256 and the ZIP
SHA-256 identify exact bytes. The baseline commit alone does not identify
editorial changes, generated PDFs, or verification-tool changes.

Logs are evidence supplements, not the main paper. Read numbered PDFs first.
